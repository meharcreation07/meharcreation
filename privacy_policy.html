Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Mehar Creation ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through meharcreation.com, why we collect it, and how we handle it. By using this website or submitting a form to us, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us — mainly through our "Send Your Requirement" enquiry form. This may include:

TypeExamples
Contact detailsYour name, email address, WhatsApp / phone number, and city
Enquiry detailsFabric required, composition, GSM, width, colour, quantity, application, and any additional requirements you type in

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to your fabric enquiry with suitable options, quotes, and availability
  • To contact you on WhatsApp, phone, or email regarding your requirement
  • To keep basic records of past enquiries for our own reference

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to any third party.

3. Who we share it with

We use the following third-party services to run this website and handle enquiries. Each only receives the minimum information needed to perform its function:

  • Formspree — processes and delivers form submissions from this site to our email inbox
  • WhatsApp / Meta — if you choose to message us via a WhatsApp link on this site, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website's files; standard web server logs (such as IP address and browser type) may be recorded automatically as part of hosting

We do not share your enquiry details with any other business or marketing service.

4. How long we keep it

We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you and maintain basic business records, and delete or anonymise it when it's no longer needed. You can request earlier deletion at any time (see Section 6).

5. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts. Your web browser may store standard technical data (such as page cache) to help pages load faster — this is normal browser behaviour, not tracking by us.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccurate information
  • Delete your information from our records

To do so, simply email or WhatsApp us using the details below — we'll act on reasonable requests within a reasonable time.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time as our website or practices change. The "last updated" date at the top will always reflect the latest version.
