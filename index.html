About Mehar Creation

From Surat
to businesses
across India.

Mehar Creation is a Surat-based textile trading and sourcing company focused on supplying quality fabrics to businesses across India.

We work with a network of textile manufacturers and suppliers to source fabrics according to the specific requirements of our customers.

Our range includes viscose, polyester, nylon, blends and other fabrics suitable for dyeing, printing and garment manufacturing.

We believe fabric sourcing is more than finding a material. It is about understanding the requirement, identifying the right quality, and delivering consistency that businesses can rely on.

Serving textile businesses across India.
