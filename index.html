About Mehar Creation

From Surat
to businesses
across India.

Mehar Creation is a Surat-based textile trading and sourcing company focused on supplying quality fabrics to businesses across India.

We work with a network of textile manufacturers and suppliers to source fabrics according to the specific requirements of our customers.

Our range includes RFD fabrics, viscose qualities, muslin, organza and other fabrics suitable for dyeing, printing and garment manufacturing.

We believe that fabric sourcing is more than simply finding a material. It is about understanding the requirement, identifying the right quality and building reliable long-term relationships.
